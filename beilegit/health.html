Medicines and healthcare

Health is where overcharging hurts most. Check what SHA covers, what a hospital may bill, and what pharmacies charge before you pay.

    Buying medicine safely

    • Medicine prices are not fixed by law in Kenya. Pharmacies charge differently for the same medicine, so compare before you buy.
    • Every licensed pharmacy displays a Health Safety Code. Send that code by SMS to 21031 (free) to confirm the pharmacy is registered with the Pharmacy and Poisons Board.
    • Ask whether a generic version is available and what it costs.
    • If you are an SHA member, ask what your cover pays before you pay cash.

    Before you pay

    • Compare the price you are quoted with the price listed here. If it is higher, ask why and show the listing.
    • Use the phone number or official link on the listing. Do not use a number given to you by a stranger or a broker.
    • Look for the Verified stamp. It means BeiLegit has confirmed the listing.
    • Always ask for a receipt that shows the amount you paid.
