Know the real price before you pay.

Organisations publish their fixed prices here. Search a product or service, compare, and pay what is listed.

    Before you pay

    • Compare the price you are quoted with the price listed here. If it is higher, ask why and show the listing.
    • Call the phone number on the listing. Do not use a number given to you by a stranger or a broker.
    • Look for the Verified stamp. It means BeiLegit has confirmed the organisation.
    • Always ask for a receipt that shows the amount you paid.

    Publish your fixed prices.

    List what you sell and what it costs. Customers who see your prices in public trust you faster.
